OmniCloud

A personal, self-hosted photo and document library.

OmniCloud runs on your own computer. It keeps one searchable library of your photos and documents, stored on your machine or in cloud accounts you connect (Google Drive, Dropbox, Koofr, pCloud). You can find a photo by describing it, or a document by what it says.

When you connect Google Drive, OmniCloud asks only for the drive.file permission: it can see and manage only the files it uploads itself, never the rest of your Drive.
