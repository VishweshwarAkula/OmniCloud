Privacy policy

Last updated: October 2026

What OmniCloud is

OmniCloud is a self-hosted application that runs only on the user's own computer. There is no OmniCloud server operated by us: we do not receive, store or process your files or personal data.

Google user data we access

If you connect Google Drive, OmniCloud requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file scope. This lets it create, read and delete only files that OmniCloud itself uploads to your Drive (inside an "OmniCloud" folder). It cannot see any other files in your Drive.

How the data is used

  • Files are uploaded to your Drive when you choose Google Drive as the destination, and read back to show them in your library.
  • To make photos and documents searchable, OmniCloud analyses them on your own computer (image and text models running locally) and stores the resulting search index on your computer.
  • OmniCloud also reads your Drive's storage quota to show how much space is left.

Storage and security

The Google authorization token is stored encrypted (AES-256-GCM) in a database on your computer. The app is only reachable from that computer (it listens on 127.0.0.1).

Sharing

Google user data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Optional: if you configure your own Google Gemini API key in OmniCloud, search queries and small image thumbnails may be sent to Google's Gemini API to improve search; without a key, nothing leaves your computer except calls to the storage services you connected.

Limited Use

OmniCloud's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access and data

Disconnect Google Drive in OmniCloud's Settings, or revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting a file in OmniCloud deletes it from your Drive and from the local index.
